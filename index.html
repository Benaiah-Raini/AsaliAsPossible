The Problem

Bees are dying while farmers wait for answers

In Bugesera District, smallholder beekeepers are losing nearly half their colonies every season. Heat stress builds inside hives undetected. Existing systems collect data but leave farmers to interpret it themselves. By the time they act, the colony is gone. 150,000 Rwandan households depend on beekeeping. They cannot afford to keep losing hives to a problem that is entirely preventable.

The Solution

From sensor to SMS in seconds

ASAP is a mobile app connected to a sensor unit inside each hive. It monitors six variables, predicts health problems before they become fatal, and sends actionable recommendations to farmers. For those without smartphones, the same recommendation arrives as a plain SMS. No one is left out.

01

Six sensors. One hive.

A hardware unit sits inside each beehive and tracks six variables continuously.

  • — Temperature
  • — Humidity
  • — Heat stress index
  • — Bee sound (acoustic)
  • — Bee movement
  • — Weight (honey yield)
02

Predictions before problems

Sensor data feeds into a CNN-LSTM model trained on 26,000 hive audio samples. It detects early stress signatures — before they become fatal. 85% prediction accuracy, validated through research at ALU.

03

Clear recommendations

The model does not just flag a problem. It tells the farmer exactly what to do. Recommendations are specific to the hive, the sensor reading, and the time of day.

"Hive Jamo is overheating. Add shade before midday."
04

App and SMS. No one left out.

Farmers with smartphones access a full mobile app — live hive data, history, and recommendations in one place. We are currently building it.

Farmers with basic phones receive the same recommendation as a plain SMS. Same information. No smartphone required.

"Hive Jamo is ready to split."

Who We Are

Built in Rwanda. Grounded in research.

ASAP grew out of final-year research at African Leadership University in Kigali. The system combines CNN-LSTM audio classification, LSTM environmental forecasting, and a reinforcement learning advisory layer. All of it delivered through a basic SMS.

We are a beVisioneers: The Mercedes-Benz Fellowship team. Our revenue model is simple. Farmers pay nothing upfront. They share a portion of the extra honey they produce. Our success is tied directly to theirs.

Beyond monitoring, ASAP is the foundation of a broader value chain. Healthier hives mean more honey. More honey means value-added products. We are building toward a Rwandan wellness brand where smallholder farmers earn at every stage — from hive to shelf.
